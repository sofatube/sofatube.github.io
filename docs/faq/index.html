FAQ

Which TVs does it work on?

Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV (the .apk; 32-bit ARM, runs on 32- and 64-bit devices), Samsung TVs from 2018 or newer (its own app, installed with Apps2Samsung; or the TizenBrew module), and LG webOS TVs through Developer Mode. Install steps.

Is it free?

Yes. Sofa Tube is a free GPL-3.0 project, with no ads and no account. Each release carries a SofaTube-<version>-source.zip on the release page; the full source is available on request.

Why is it not in the TV's app store?

Sofa Tube opens YouTube's own TV page and adds features on top (ad blocking among them), so it is installed from outside the store: with the Downloader app on Android, Apps2Samsung (or TizenBrew) on Samsung and Developer Mode on LG.

How do updates work?
  • Android: install the new .apk over this one the same way; settings stay.
  • Samsung: Settings → Backup and about → Check for updates (it updates on the TV when it can; otherwise install the new file with Apps2Samsung the same way; settings stay). The TizenBrew module updates itself.
  • LG: Settings → Backup and about → Check for updates (with Sofa Tube Phone), or install the new .ipk files the same way; your settings stay. Update Sofa Tube Phone too when a release says so (0.9.5 does): how.

Nothing is checked unless you ask (or turn on the weekly check). The short links (sofatube.github.io/tv, /lg, /lg-phone, /samsung) always point to the latest release.

Do I need an account? Where are my settings kept?

No account is needed: follow channels without signing in (My channels). Settings and the kids' watch history stay on the TV. The phone page is served by the TV itself on your Wi-Fi; the PIN stays on the TV and is never in a backup.

What goes online, and when: What goes online?

What goes online?

Only what a feature needs, and most of it only when you turn it on or ask for it:

  • YouTube, for everything you watch. Mark the most replayed part on the seek bar (off by default) and Jump to the most replayed part ask youtube.com too.
  • SponsorBlock, Return YouTube Dislike and DeArrow (community services), for the video you watch.
  • Lyrics (Action menu, only when you ask): LRCLIB (lrclib.net) gets only the artist, the song and its length.
  • Updates: only when you press Check for updates or turn on the weekly check (from sofatube.github.io, or its jsDelivr copy). The same file can carry a switch-off list that turns a feature off when a YouTube change breaks it, until a fix is out: data only, never code. Diagnostics shows what and why, and “Follow Sofa Tube's switch-off list” can be turned off.
  • Control from away and remembered helpers (both off until you set them up): sealed messages through ntfy.sh or your own server. Who can see what.
  • Remote help: the two phones connect directly; to find each other they ask a public STUN server. What goes where.

Stays at home: the phone page (the TV serves it on your Wi-Fi), Share with another TV and My TVs (no server), photos and videos shown from the phone (kept on the TV only briefly, deleted when the viewer closes), home automation (home network only), the Wi-Fi check and the “YouTube changed something” check.

Control from away: who can see what?

Control from away is off by default. A parent turns it on at the TV (Settings → Kids → Control from away; a PIN is needed) and adds a phone at home by scanning the TV's code. Both screens show the same 4 words; then OK and the PIN on the TV.

  • An end-to-end encrypted mailbox. The TV and the phone leave sealed messages for each other on a free public ntfy server, ntfy.sh by default. Only your TV and your phone have the keys. The server sees the internet addresses of the TV and the phone, when a message is left and its size (always one of three fixed sizes), never what is in it: not the video, not the app, not the time used, not your messages. The TV only calls out; nothing on the internet can call into your TV.
  • The server can be changed: run your own ntfy server and type its address on the TV (Settings → Kids → Own mailbox server); paired phones move to it by themselves. Or use your own Tailscale: the phone is then “at home” and no mailbox server is used. The guide, in plain words: How to connect from away.
  • Kids' requests (“more time”, “can I watch this?”, “can I open this app?”) reach the parent's phone as a notification that the TV itself sends through the phone's own notification service (Apple, Google or Mozilla). That service also gets only a sealed message.
  • “Now” shows only what is on at that moment, made when the parent asks; no history is kept. While Control from away is on, the TV shows a small sign (“A parent's phone can see what's on now”) and a short message each time a parent looks.
  • Some things stay at home: changing the PIN, removing phones and turning Control from away off can only be done on the TV. Turning the TV off from away needs “Control the whole TV from the phone”. If the internet is down, the rules keep working on the TV.
Remote help: what goes where?

Remote help lets someone you trust help from far away, phone to phone. It starts on the family's side: Get help on the phone page makes a code to send to the helper (WhatsApp or another app), the helper's answer comes back the same way, and both phones show the same 4 pictures to check. The helper uses sofatube.github.io/help.

  • No server of ours: the two phones connect directly. To find a way between two homes, both phones ask a public STUN server (by default stun.l.google.com:19302, Google's) for their own public address; it learns that address and nothing else. In the help card's Connection settings you can turn this off (then only on the same Wi-Fi), use your own STUN server, or add your own TURN server.
  • The TV asks first, with the remote, what the helper may do (see, remote keys and Up next, settings, Fix it) for 30 minutes, shows a banner the whole time and keeps a list of what was changed. Back twice or Stop ends it. Kid rules change only with the PIN on the TV.
  • Remembered helpers: after a session the family can remember the helper, and next time “Ask … for help” connects without copying codes, through the same end-to-end encrypted mailbox as Control from away (ntfy.sh, or your own ntfy server). The mailbox sees only the phones' addresses, and nothing stays there beyond 12 hours. The TV still asks every time, and either side can forget.
  • The TV's diagnostics and a Fix it result go to the helper only after you have seen them. The helper sees the TV through the relative's phone camera only when the relative taps it, and it stops at any time.
Known limits in 0.9.5

LG

  • After a TV restart or a full power-off, open Sofa Tube Phone once on the TV: Control from away and the family plan's bedtime and allowed apps wait until then (Sofa Tube itself can't start the Phone app's helper on LG). Use the TV's own app lock as well (Settings → Safety).
  • “Ask for more time” on the bedtime warning is checked on the LG emulator, not yet on a real LG TV. “Even out loud and quiet parts” is not yet tried on a real LG TV.

Samsung (Whole TV)

  • Kids' allowed apps work for the common apps only (Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV, Spotify, Internet); other apps are never closed, so use the TV's own app lock as well. YouTube is always allowed, since Sofa Tube runs inside it. A daily limit per app: common apps only too.
  • No rules for HDMI inputs (game consoles, boxes): Samsung doesn't let apps see HDMI.
  • Reminders and a routine's message show only inside Sofa Tube, not over other apps. “Ask a parent” on a blocked app shows only on Sofa Tube's own screen.
  • “On the TV now” is hidden on TVs whose app interface does not answer (the 2023 and newer TVs in Samsung's Remote Test Lab answered “not found” for every app).
  • The TV volume number and the kids volume cap work only where the TV's network volume control answers, and are not yet confirmed on a real Samsung TV. Whole-TV pairing and the Soundbar badge also still need a check on a Samsung TV at home (the lab TVs refused new remote connections).
  • No Next / Previous keys on the phone (Samsung has none). No most replayed part (YouTube does not send it to Samsung TVs), and “Even out loud and quiet parts” is hidden (the TV's sound can't be processed).

All TVs

  • “Play the adhan or a playlist” at prayer time and reminders for lives and premieres work only while Sofa Tube is open.
  • Android TV: the family plan's volume cap, allowed apps and prayer mute were checked on a real Android TV; turning the TV off at bedtime was not tried there. “Even out loud and quiet parts” is hidden (the TV's sound can't be processed).
  • “Hide YouTube's extra Home rows” is not yet seen on a real TV (the rows show only for signed-in accounts). “Fill the screen” is checked on a real Android TV, not yet on a real LG or Samsung picture.
  • iPhone: Control from away pairs and gets notifications only inside the Home Screen app (notifications need iOS 16.4 or newer).
  • Remote help does not always connect (about 1 in 5 tries fail, more on mobile data); a WhatsApp video call pointed at the TV works too.
Can it harm my TV?

Nothing here can harm your TV; at worst a feature does not work. On LG, uninstall the official YouTube app first (Sofa Tube uses the same app ID).

Troubleshooting
  • No colour buttons? Use ★ Sofa Tube in YouTube's sidebar or the ★ button in the player bar.
  • LG: the apps stopped opening? The Developer Mode session expired: open the Developer Mode app and extend it.
  • Sound cuts out at some speeds? Playback → Speed method → Re-timing.
  • Prayer times wrong? Set the TV to the right time zone.
  • The phone can't connect? The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. If the TV's address changed, sofatube.github.io/remote finds the TV again (on Chrome) or opens the last address.
  • Android: won't install? Allow Downloader to install unknown apps.
