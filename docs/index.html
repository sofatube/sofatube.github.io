Whole TV

Your phone controls the whole TV

Not only YouTube: the TV's home screen, its other apps, volume, inputs, typing and turning it off, from the phone already in your hand.

  • Keys that work anywhere: arrows, OK, Back, Home, Menu, Exit, volume and channels, even with Sofa Tube in the background.
  • Apps and inputs: open any app on the TV, or switch to another device connected to it.
  • Type on the TV: when a text box opens on the TV, type on your phone.
  • Touchpad and air mouse: move a pointer on the screen, like LG's Magic Remote.
  • For parents: off at first and turned on in Sofa Tube's settings; a kids phone never gets it.

0.9.4 is a test version. It was tried on TV emulators (software copies of a TV) and on a few real TVs (one Android TV, three Samsung TVs), not yet on every brand and model. What has been tested.

New in 0.9.5

0.9.5 adds family rules for the whole TV, control from away, the phone as a second screen, remote help, and easier screens for elderly viewers.

⏻

Family plan for the whole TV

Kids' daily TV time across every app (a warning 5 minutes and 1 minute before, then the TV turns off), a bedtime that can turn the TV off, a sleep timer, allowed apps only, a maximum volume for kids, and a pause in other apps at prayer time. More time only from a parent. Needs “Control the whole TV from the phone”; off by default.

✉

Control from away

A parent's phone outside the home sees what's on now and sends more time, a lock, turn off, a message, bedtime tonight or an allowed app. Kids' requests arrive as notifications. Sealed end to end; the TV shows a sign when a parent looks. Off by default. Who sees what.

▭

The phone as a second screen

Search results on the phone while the TV keeps playing (Play now or Up next), share this moment, the description, comments (not for kids) and large captions in step with the TV. A guest phone link for visitors: arrows, search and Up next, no settings.

⚇

Remote help from someone you trust

A son or a niece helps from far away, phone to phone. The TV first asks, with the remote, what they may do for 30 minutes, shows a banner the whole time and lists what was changed; Back twice ends it. Kid rules only with the PIN. Also “Watch together” and Fix it.

Aa

Easier to see and use

Ready-made settings (Kids, Elderly viewers, Music, Night, Listen) that can be undone, Simple mode for elderly viewers (a thick yellow focus frame, only the main buttons act), text size Large or Larger, and High contrast for Sofa Tube's own screens.

☾

Next prayer and Hijri date

The next prayer by the clock (iftar and imsak in Ramadan) and the Hijri date, with a correction of up to 2 days. At prayer time, Sofa Tube can play the adhan or a playlist you chose, then bring the video back paused (while Sofa Tube is open).

⟲

More control while watching

Not interested and Don't recommend this channel, My playlists (no account needed), Repeat a part (A–B), Saved moments, and SponsorBlock choices: ask before skipping, mute marked parts instead, leave short parts alone. Lyrics only when asked.

⇆

Share with another TV

Send settings, My channels, Watch later, My playlists or kids rules to another TV with a share code. The other TV shows what will change first; kids rules need its PIN. No server.

⎚

HDMI devices and soundbar

Named inputs (“HDMI 2 · PlayStation 5”), remote keys to devices on HDMI through HDMI-CEC, and a volume that knows when a soundbar or receiver plays the sound. Fix it unmutes, sets the usual volume and input, and says what it fixed.

Some parts are not yet confirmed on every real TV, and what each brand can do differs: known limits. On LG, update the Sofa Tube Phone app too: how.

What it does

It opens YouTube's own TV page and adds these features on top.

⛨

Ads and SponsorBlock

Ads are blocked. SponsorBlock skips sponsor parts, intros and more, using community data.

▶

Playback speed

0.25× to 3×, normal pitch. Press Red while watching and pick a speed.

⚿

Locks and PIN

Set a PIN on the Locks page. It protects the settings, the locks and the kids options. Lock the app, or lock to one video.

⌂

Kid mode

Turn it on, then choose: family-safe only, longest video, hide search, kids list only, allowed hours and break reminder.

☾

Prayer times

Umm al-Qura, worked out on the TV. Choose a reminder only, or pause the video at the adhan.

▣

Settings from your phone

Scan the code with a phone on the same Wi-Fi. The phone can change settings, search, play links, control playback and make backups. The PIN stays on the TV.

And also

  • ★My lists: Follow channels without signing in (My channels) and see their new videos. Save videos in Watch later.
  • ≡Feed filters: Hide Shorts, mixes, live, watched or old videos, blocked words and channels.
  • ◐Night dim and screen off: Night dim darkens the picture, all the time or between two times. Screen off keeps the sound playing; any button turns it back on.
  • ☆The ★ Sofa Tube menu: For remotes without colour buttons: ★ Sofa Tube in YouTube's sidebar or the ★ button in the player bar.
  • ⇥Jump and chapters: Number keys 1–9 jump to 10%–90%, 0 back to start. CH+ / CH− next or previous chapter.
  • ⌗Learned buttons: Any spare button can run an action: press a button, then choose its action.
  • ⇄Backup: Export shows a code to photograph; Import restores it. The PIN is never included.
  • عEnglish and Arabic: English and Arabic, with Arabic right to left.

Remote buttons

  • Green: settings
  • Red: speed (while watching)
  • Yellow: Action menu
  • Blue: audio only

1–9 jump to 10%–90%, 0 back to start · CH+ / CH− next / previous chapter · hold a colour button: what every button does.

No colour buttons? Use ★ Sofa Tube in YouTube's sidebar, the ★ button in the player bar, or Sofa Tube at the top of YouTube's Settings page.
